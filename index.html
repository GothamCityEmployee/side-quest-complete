Our Story

Side Quest Complete is a retro toy, game, and collectables shop born out of a lifelong obsession with the golden eras of gaming and pop culture. We're based in New Bedford, MA — and we're serious about the stuff we sell.

Every item in our inventory is handpicked, tested, and genuinely loved before it hits the shelf. We're not a warehouse operation. We're collectors who happen to have too much good stuff and want to find it the right homes.

Whether you're chasing a childhood memory or building a serious collection, we've got something for you. Stop by, dig through, and complete your side quest.
