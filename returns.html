Side Quest Complete LLC sells vintage, used, retro, and collectible merchandise. The condition, age, and characteristics of each item are described to the best of our ability at the time of listing. Due to the nature of vintage and pre-owned goods, all sales are final and we do not accept returns or issue refunds except in the limited circumstances described below.

No Returns & No Refunds

  • All purchases are final at the time of checkout
  • We do not accept returns for any reason including change of mind, buyer's remorse, or duplicate purchase
  • Refunds will not be issued for items that match their listed description and condition

Item Not as Described

We stand behind the accuracy of our listings. In the rare event that an item is materially different from its listed description (e.g., wrong item shipped, significant undisclosed damage), you must contact us within 48 hours of delivery at sidequestcompletellc@gmail.com with your order number and photos documenting the issue. We will review each claim individually and, at our sole discretion, may offer a replacement, store credit, or partial refund.
